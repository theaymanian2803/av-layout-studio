# Pet food storefront redesign

## Goal
Transform the current camera-equipment shop into a pet food and supplies store inspired by the supplied reference, while keeping accounts, cart, checkout, PayPal, wishlist, coupons, orders, contact messages, admin tools, popup, and editable landing sections.

## Storefront redesign
- Rebrand the customer-facing store with a bright sky-blue, sunny yellow, coral, and white retail palette.
- Rebuild the header as a compact utility bar, pet-store logo/search row, and blue category navigation while preserving mega-menu controls.
- Recompose the homepage with:
  - A large dog-food promotion and two smaller offers
  - Circular illustrated pet categories
  - “This Week’s Highlights” and “Best Selling Items” product rows
  - Two promotional image banners
  - Department product lists
  - Shipping, returns, support, and payment benefits
  - Customer testimonials and pet-care journal cards
  - Newsletter and expanded retail footer
- Restyle product cards, prices, badges, buttons, spacing, and mobile layouts.
- Update customer-facing wording and page metadata from camera equipment to pet food and supplies.

## Catalog data
- Replace the current demo catalog with realistic dog, cat, fish, bird, and small-pet products.
- Seed matching brands, categories, subcategories, prices, sale prices, ratings, stock, descriptions, specifications, and homepage sections.
- Generate a cohesive set of pet and product images; the supplied screenshot remains a visual reference only.

## Existing functionality retained
- Authentication and customer accounts
- Cart, checkout, PayPal, cash on delivery, coupons, and confirmation emails
- Favorites, reviews, order history, addresses, and saved payment methods
- Admin products, inventory, brands, categories, orders, contacts, imports, coupons, popup, mega menu, and landing-page controls

## Technical details
- Keep the current database structure and security rules; refresh only catalog and landing-page records.
- Preserve existing routes and admin workflows.
- Use semantic design tokens for light and dark themes.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, product links, add-to-cart actions, image loading, and runtime health.
